Privacy Policy — APB Leonida Uploader

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Who uses this tool

APB Leonida Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels APB Leonida and APB Leonida DE. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Your data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

  • Upload (scope youtube.upload): to upload approved videos and thumbnails to the owner's channel, as private.
  • Read (scope youtube.readonly): to read the channel name and the titles of existing uploads, so that a video is not uploaded twice.

No other Google or YouTube data is accessed.

How data is stored and shared

The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the owner's own computer and never leaves it except in requests to Google. Channel data is used only during the upload and is not stored, sold or shared with any third party. No cookies or analytics are used.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
